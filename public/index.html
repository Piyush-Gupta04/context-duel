  1. Pick a difficulty, create a game and send your friend the link plus the 4-letter code.
  2. Take turns. Each word you type gets a rank by how close its meaning is to the secret word.
  3. Every word in the list is ranked out of 30,000. Rank 1 is the secret word itself.
  4. Stuck? A hint adds a closer word to your list, all the way down to rank 2. It never uses your turn.
